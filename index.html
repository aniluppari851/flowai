1. Problem Understanding

The Urban Traffic Challenge

In modern metropolitan areas, urban road networks operate under continuous strain. Traffic conditions do not remain static; they evolve rapidly and unpredictably throughout the day. A corridor moving smoothly at one moment can plunge into gridlock minutes later due to:

⚡ Asymmetric Peak Demand

Sudden morning and evening surges where vehicle volume exceeds the physical capacity of major arterial corridors and flyovers.

🚦 Signal Platoon Delays

Periodic traffic signal cycles that repeatedly compress vehicles into dense platoons, creating recurring friction points at intersections.

🚧 Incidents & Roadworks

Collisions, vehicle breakdowns, and maintenance closures that suddenly throttle capacity, launching backward-propagating shockwaves.

🔄 Cascading Queue Spillback

Queues exceeding link storage back up into upstream intersection boxes, physically blocking lateral traffic and locking entire grids.

Why Simply Knowing a Road is Congested is Not Enough

Conventional traffic monitoring tools only report that a road is currently "red" or delayed. For traffic managers and urban planners, this notification arrives too late and provides no actionable path forward. Decision-makers need an intelligence system that answers five fundamental questions:

Core Question Operational Meaning FlowSight AI Capability
1. What is happening? Continuous Situational Awareness Estimates true traffic conditions, velocities, and service levels across all connected road segments.
2. Why is it happening? Causal Attribution Determines whether slowdowns stem from peak volume, collisions, roadworks, or upstream spillback.
3. What will happen next? Multi-Horizon Forecasting Predicts how congestion and queues will spread over the next 15, 30, 45, and 60 minutes.
4. What action to simulate? Tactical Diversion Evaluation Simulates rerouting traffic onto alternate corridors to relieve chokepoints without overloading secondary roads.
5. How to permanently improve? Strategic Infrastructure Planning Identifies recurring structural bottlenecks and simulates candidate geometry changes (lanes, turn pockets).
System Purpose & Operating Boundaries
FlowSight AI is built strictly on organizer-provided traffic observations and road-network datasets. It is a decision-support and simulation platform. It does NOT control traffic signals, access live municipal CCTV feeds, track individual GPS devices, tap roadside sensors, access municipal intranets, or authorize real-world construction.

2. How the Application Works

When a judge, traffic operator, or planner opens FlowSight AI, the platform provides a complete, user-facing operational experience designed to turn raw data into clear situational understanding and simulated action.

2.1 Traffic Command Center

Upon launching the application, the user is presented with the unified **Traffic Command Center**:

  • Interactive Network Map: Complete topological road network rendering directed segments color-coded by real-time health.
  • Current Traffic Conditions: Instantaneous velocity, estimated travel times, and road service levels across every arterial link.
  • Configurable Congestion Regimes: Segments categorized into Free-Flow, Building, Congested, Severe, or Unknown / Low Confidence.
  • Live Alerts & Data Quality: Prioritized alert panel showing active anomalies, observation freshness, and confidence scores.
  • Replay Time Controller: Slider allowing users to step through live streaming batches or replay historical traffic days.

2.2 Traffic Intelligence & Evidence Dossiers

FlowSight AI compares current segment speeds against time-of-day and day-of-week historical baselines to decouple expected peak congestion from abnormal disruptions. When an alert is raised, clicking the segment opens an **Evidence Dossier** detailing the speed drop magnitude, expected historical speed, duration, and neighboring road status.

2.3 Multi-Horizon Traffic Forecasting (15–60 Minutes)

An interactive **Forecast Slider** allows users to view anticipated network states across four planning horizons:

  • 15 Minutes: Identifies immediate shockwave inception and approaching platoon bottlenecks.
  • 30 Minutes: Highlights the critical operational window to evaluate diversions before upstream junctions lock up.
  • 45 to 60 Minutes: Reveals whether a corridor will naturally dissipate or trigger cascading gridlock across the network.

2.4 Incident Intelligence & Attribution

When abnormal traffic behavior occurs, the system correlates the kinematic drop against organizer incident and roadwork logs. If corroborated by a roadwork permit, it is marked as a *Confirmed Roadwork Bottleneck*. If uncorroborated by available dataset records, the system strictly tags it as an "Incident-Like Anomaly"—never inventing an accident cause without verified data.

2.5 Spillback & Network Impact

As vehicle queues approach segment physical capacity, the application traces connected upstream feeder links. It flags intersections where queues threaten to block lateral cross-street traffic, alerting operators of impending gridlock cascades.

2.7 Bottleneck Intelligence & Infrastructure What-If Simulation

For long-term planning, FlowSight AI mines multi-week observation histories to isolate recurring structural choke points, evaluating frequency, duration, and delay.

In the **Infrastructure Studio**, planners test candidate modifications (adding lanes, dedicated turn pockets, lane allocation shifts, turn restrictions, or new connector links). The application renders side-by-side **Before vs. After** metrics (total vehicle hours traveled, corridor delay, queue dissipation times) to guide capital investment.

Application Component Roles in Simple Terms

Application Component Simple Functional Role & User Experience Purpose
Data Layer Ingests and normalizes organizer traffic observations, road network graph, signal plans, and incident logs, attaching them to the network digital twin.
Intelligence Layer Transforms observations into understandable traffic conditions (Free-flow, Building, Congested, Severe), flags anomalies, and evaluates incident likelihood.
Forecasting Layer Provides rolling 15, 30, 45, and 60-minute future traffic states, giving operators time to intervene before gridlock occurs.
Simulation Layer Risk-free sandbox testing diversion rerouting and hypothetical infrastructure changes (lanes, turn pockets) under identical demand.
Decision Support Layer Synthesizes outputs into clear, evidence-based advisories detailing supporting indicators, expected savings, and confidence scores.
Application Layer (UI) The primary Command Center interface where users inspect the live network, adjust forecast sliders, test diversions, and explore scenarios.
Automation Layer (n8n) The workflow orchestrator that schedules pipeline updates, monitors threshold triggers, and dispatches alerts. (Not the AI model itself)
Database Layer (Supabase) The persistent relational state store managing network topology, observations, forecasts, incident cards, simulation runs, and advisories.
